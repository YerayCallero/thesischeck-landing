2

China Revenue Share & Export Control Normalization Cliff

WARNING — CYCLICAL REVENUE CLIFF
Thesis Catalyst Requirement

Revenues from Chinese semiconductor manufacturers must represent a sustainable baseline without creating an artificial demand bubble driven by fear of export bans.

SEC 20-F Findings (Note 19 & Item 3.D Risk Factors)
  • • FY2024 China Share of Net System Sales: 41.0% (vs. 29% in 2023 and 14% in 2022)
  • • Mechanism: Chinese domestic fabs (SMIC, CXMT) pre-bought mid-tier immersion DUV tools (NXT:1980/2000 series) prior to export restrictions
  • • Management Guidance in 20-F: China revenue expected to normalize down to ~20% of net sales in 2026
  • • Regulatory Risk: Dutch & US rules prohibit servicing/maintenance of certain installed advanced DUV tools.
Audit Verdict: The 41% revenue spike was an artificial pre-buying buffer. The contraction to ~20% creates a multi-billion euro revenue headwind that EUV growth must fully absorb.
